Two questions for the room.

What do we need to answer to ship Fernie — and what do we want Fernie to become?

To ship Fernie

Decisions needed before a customer sees it 4 questions
Q1 · NAME
What do we call it?
Fernie Cora "Chat" Something else
Fernie = our brand DNA. Cora = neutral coworker. "Chat" = surrender the moat.
Q2 · SCOPE
What does Fernie do at v1?
Read & suggest Take actions w/ confirm Autonomous
Today Fernie can navigate & filter grids. Moving to "take actions" needs review-then-execute UX.
Q3 · PRIORITY
Where does this rank on the roadmap?
Top-three this year A real bet, not the top Slow track
Q3 2026 ship is still realistic if it's a top-three bet — PRODUCE Web wraps S28 (Jun 9), gives ~6 weeks runway before Jul 1. Lower priority pushes ship to Q4 or beyond.
Q4 · COST MODEL
Who pays for the model calls?
BYOK — customer brings their key We handle it — bundled in pricing Hybrid (free tier + BYOK above)
BYOK = zero cost to us, friction for the customer. Bundled = clean UX, variable margin we have to manage. Hybrid = best of both, more complexity to build.

The future of Fernie

The bets we're being asked to make 6 questions
F1 · AMBITION
If Fernie ships and a grower says "it's just like ChatGPT" — have we won or lost?
Polished assistant Co-pilot Autopilot
Assistant = parity with ChatGPT. Co-pilot = something they can't get anywhere else. Autopilot = a category we'd invent.
F2 · PRODUCT SHAPE
Every customer is already paying ChatGPT $20 a month. Why aren't they paying us?
Embedded in WorkSuite Premium tier Standalone product
Free in the platform = invisible value. Premium tier = a line item to defend. Standalone = a sales motion to build.
F3 · CONSULTING
Our customers are paying someone for AI help right now. If it's not us, who's earning trust we used to own?
No Pure service Productized packages Wedge into platform
Pure service eats engineer time. Productized packages scale. Wedge = consulting walks them onto the platform.
F4 · CROSS-PRODUCT
A grower uses three of our products in a day. Should Fernie remember them across all three — or start over each time?
WorkSuite PWA only PWA + Produce desktop Everywhere (mobile too)
PWA only = one surface. PWA + Produce = unify the brain across both apps. Everywhere = mobile, email, voice.
F5 · BUILD VS. PARTNER
If our AI brain belongs to Anthropic, is the strategy actually ours — or theirs?
Vendor model + our logic Our own model Hybrid over time
Vendor + our logic = standard play, lowest risk, the data and workflows stay ours. Our own model = years and millions. Hybrid = optionality.
F6 · NORTH STAR
If we can't point to a number that proves Fernie works, are we shipping software or theater?
Adoption % Hours saved per customer NPS lift Revenue attached
Adoption tells us if anyone tried it. Hours saved tells us if anyone keeps using it. NPS + revenue tell us if it mattered.
What we leave with today

Answers to all four ship questions, plus F1. Everything else can wait.

Name. v1 scope. Priority. Cost model. Ambition ceiling. With those five decided, the rest of the roadmap writes itself. Without them, every conversation about Fernie loops back to the same place.
